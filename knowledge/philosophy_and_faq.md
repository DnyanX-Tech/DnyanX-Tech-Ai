# DnyanX Tech - Agency Philosophy & FAQ

## Business Philosophy & Core Principles

At DnyanX Tech, we operate on three foundational principles:

1. **Value Equation**: `Value = Pain × People × Frequency`
   - We focus on solving high-frequency, high-pain problems for large groups of users.
2. **Value First Selling**: *"Sell the hole, not the drill machine"*
   - Clients buy outcomes and efficiency, not just code frameworks.
3. **Trust Formula**: `Trust = Value + Consistency + Authenticity`
   - Transparent communication, on-time delivery, and authentic client partnership.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How long does it take to deploy a custom AI assistant?
**A**: Starter AI assistants take 7-10 business days. Custom enterprise solutions with deep integrations take 2-4 weeks.

### Q: Can DnyanX AI integrate with our existing database?
**A**: Yes! We support PostgreSQL, Supabase, MySQL, MongoDB, and vector stores like pgvector, Pinecone, or Qdrant.

### Q: What LLM models do you support?
**A**: OpenAI GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude 3.5, GLM-4.6, Llama 3, and open-source models hosted locally or on vLLM.
